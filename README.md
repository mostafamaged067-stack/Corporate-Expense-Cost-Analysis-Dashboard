# 📊 Corporate Expense & Cost Analysis Dashboard

An advanced, interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze corporate expenses, highlight financial KPIs, and support data-driven decision-making across departments.


---

# 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard analyzes more than **49M+** in total expenses and extracts essential financial metrics:

| Metric | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Total Amount** | **49,769,224.87** |
| **Max Transaction** | **499,819.83** |
| **Min Transaction** | **-49,970.45** |
| **Average Spending** | **197,105.84** |
| **Top Spending Department** | **12,821,933.49** |

---

# 🧩 Dashboard Components

## **1️⃣ Department Spending**
Breakdown of total spending across departments:
- Finance  
- Operations  
- R&D  
- Sales  

Includes vertical bar charts & dynamic department filtering.

---

## **2️⃣ Expense Distribution by Category**
Horizontal bar chart showing which cost categories drive most of the spending.

---

## **3️⃣ Account Type Breakdown**
A pie chart covering 5 financial account types:
- COGS  
- SG&A  
- R&D  
- Revenue  
- Other  

---

## **4️⃣ Monthly Spending Trend**
A multi-year line chart visualizing spending changes over time to monitor trends and anomalies.

---

## **5️⃣ Total Amount by Currency**
Bar chart comparing currency-wise expenses:
- **EGP**
- **EUR**
- **GBP**
- **USD**

---

## **6️⃣ Top 10 Highest Transactions**
A clear ranked list visualizing the largest individual expense transactions.
